Which devices can use Word Wise?

Egosa format ― Sample output (researched: Nov 9, 2026)

Question

Word Wise on Kindle looks useful. But which devices can use it, and which cannot?

Conclusion

Word Wise works on three kinds of devices only: Kindle e-readers (6th generation or later), Fire tablets (3rd generation or later), and the Kindle app for Android. It does not work on iPhone, iPad, PC, Mac, or in the browser. And not every book supports it. You can check a book before you buy it, on its Amazon product page.

Details

Word Wise shows a short, simple definition above difficult words while you read. You do not have to stop and open a dictionary, so your reading keeps flowing.

Amazon says it like this:

“Word Wise is only available in books in English and isn’t enabled on all Kindle books. You can verify whether the feature is available by checking for “Word Wise: Enabled” on the product detail page.”

Two points matter here. First, only English books can have Word Wise, and even then, not all of them do. Second, you can check before you buy. On Amazon.co.jp, the product details have a Word Wise row. It shows 有効 (enabled) or 有効にされていません (not enabled).

On a Kindle e-reader, you turn on Word Wise from the Aa menu while a book is open. A slider lets you choose how many hints you see. On an iPhone, there is no setting for it at all.

So the rule is simple: a supported device plus a supported book. If you miss one of the two, Word Wise stays silent.
